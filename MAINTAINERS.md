## Becoming a maintainer

There is no formal maintainer track yet. Sustained contributions to code,
documentation, tests or data governance are reviewed case by case. A future
maintainer is expected to understand the provenance invariants, the public-data
policy and the release checklist before receiving write access.

## Response expectations

This is a volunteer research-software project. Security reports are prioritized;
other issues are handled on a best-effort basis. No response-time SLA is offered.
