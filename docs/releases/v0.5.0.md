# ntx v0.5.0 — 2026-11-14

Audit scoring and gold-integrity gates. Two ways a metric could previously be
computed from unchecked data are now explicit, reported and testable. Rules-only
core; still no human gold metric.

## Highlights

- **`ntx audit score`.** A completed verdict file is joined to claims and yields
  precision with a 95% Wilson interval, overall and by role and design.
  `unclear` and blank verdicts are excluded from the denominator and reported;
  verdicts for missing claim ids are counted as a warning rather than silently
  dropped. `support`/`refute` are the only decisive labels.
- **A closed audit loop.** `ntx audit sample --verdicts-out` writes a blank
  machine-readable template next to the human worksheet. Fill `verdict` with
  `support`, `refute` or `unclear`, then run `ntx audit score`. The sample is
  still deterministic and stratified by role/design.
- **Strict verdict validation.** Malformed JSON, an unknown verdict or a
  duplicate `claim_id` fails with a file/line error instead of producing a
  partial score.
- **Gold integrity in `ntx eval`.** Every gold claim sentence and mention span is
  checked against the stored document text. The report exposes
  `gold_span_errors`, `gold_missing_documents` and capped
  `gold_error_examples`; `--strict-gold` exits 1 when either count is non-zero.
  Invalid or absent gold can no longer silently produce a metric.
- **Wilson interval in the stdlib.** `ntx.stats.wilson_interval` keeps small
  audit intervals inside `[0, 1]` and behaves at 0/n; no new dependency.

## Not in this release

- Human gold v2 (30 documents, double annotation, adjudication) is still pending,
  so no v2 accuracy metric is claimed; the audit score is a descriptive check of
  model output, not a substitute.
- Docker image and Zenodo DOI require Docker and a public remote.
- An LLM extractor remains a deferred, opt-in idea (ADR 0002).
