# ntx v0.6.0 — 2026-11-14

Mechanistic layer: a signed graph and qualitative perturbation simulation,
explicitly framed as hypothesis triage rather than mechanism discovery.

## Highlights

- **A mechanistic graph with two distinguishable sources.** A curated prior of
  22 ligand→receptor→pathway→process nodes and 40 signed edges (textbook-level
  assumptions, not citations), plus entity→mechanism and mechanism→outcome edges
  extracted from sentences with doc provenance. Every exported edge says whether
  it is prior, claim, or both. Mechanism→outcome edges retain the claim's entity,
  so paths cannot be stitched across different peptides.
- **Extraction that can say no.** A direction cue must occur in the same clause;
  negation immediately before the cue vetoes the edge; `via/through/associated`
  becomes a sign-0 edge that is displayed but never simulated; receptor names
  that overlap an entity mention ("substance P receptor") are skipped.
- **`ntx mechanism simulate`.** Blocks one or more mechanism nodes, removes every
  path through them, and reports baseline/perturbed/delta **directions** per
  outcome. Paths are simple, depth-limited and confidence-weighted. The output
  states that this is not kinetic, quantitative, or a prediction of effect size.
- **`ntx mechanism check`.** Deterministic SHA-1 holdout (20% by default): the
  graph is rebuilt without held-out documents, then predicted baseline signs are
  compared with held-out claim directions. `--holdout 0` is labelled in-sample.
  Agreement is a weak structural check, not validation.
- **`ntx mechanism export` and `edges`.** Inspect the whole graph or only the
  extracted edges with support and doc ids.
- **`ntx mechanism enrich`.** Opt-in UniProtKB metadata through the normal HTTP
  cache; no edges are added automatically and `--offline` uses cache only.
- **Runnable example.** `examples/mechanism_corpus.json` exercises the layer on
  synthetic mechanism sentences; it is not a literature sample.

## Not in this release

- Quantitative kinetics, parameter fitting, dose/route or effect-size prediction.
- Candidate/sequence generation, docking, or claims of new molecules.
- Automatic import of Reactome/STRING edges; online enrichment is UniProt
  metadata only.
- Human gold v2 and any validated mechanism claim.

A graph path is an assumption chain, not evidence that the mechanism is real.
