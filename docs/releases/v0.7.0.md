# ntx v0.7.0 — 2026-11-14

Closed-loop verification: candidates can be recorded, predictions are registered
before outcomes, corrections stay append-only, and results are scored with
explicit exclusions. This release does not generate molecules and does not turn a
small ledger into calibration evidence.

## Highlights

- **Schema v4.** New `candidates`, `experiments`, `predictions` and `outcomes`
  tables. SQLite triggers reject `UPDATE` and `DELETE`; corrections are new rows.
  A v3 database migrates in place without losing claims or documents.
- **`ntx candidate import|list|show`.** Transactional JSONL/JSON import with
  canonical-residue, length and target checks; content-derived `C-...` ids;
  `--dry-run`; residue-table descriptors (MW, net charge at pH 7.4, GRAVY,
  aromatic/cysteine counts, simple protease motifs). Every descriptor object says
  `heuristic: true` and that modifications were not applied.
- **`ntx ledger register`.** Resolves a hypothesis from `ntx discover`, stores a
  snapshot of it, and writes one experiment plus one write-once prediction.
  Probability must be in `[0.5, 1.0)`; design, assay and primary outcome are
  required.
- **`ntx ledger outcome`.** Append-only result capture with effect size, CI, n,
  QC and notes. The latest outcome per experiment is scored; all revisions remain
  exported.
- **`ntx ledger score`.** Directional Brier score, skill versus 0.25, Wilson 95%
  accuracy interval, per-kind/per-design descriptive groups, pending/excluded
  counts and an underpowered flag below 10 eligible observations. `qc=fail`,
  `none`, `mixed` and `unclear` are excluded, not imputed.
- **`ntx ledger export`.** Canonical JSON with a SHA-256 content hash over every
  table; only `exported_at` is excluded, so repeated exports of the same ledger
  share a hash.
- **`docs/LEDGER.md`.** Protocol, formula, exclusions and the trust boundary:
  the ledger records timestamps and an optional preregistration id but cannot
  prove a prediction preceded the experiment.

## Not in this release

- Sequence generation, truncation, precursor cleavage, docking or ranking.
- Activity, potency, efficacy, BBB or safety prediction.
- Power calculation, automatic outcome extraction, or remote registry sync.
- Calibration claims from small or selectively reported ledgers.
- Any claim that an imported candidate is novel or active.

The next candidate layer is precursor cleavage/analog enumeration with explicit
generator provenance and the same pre-registration rules.
